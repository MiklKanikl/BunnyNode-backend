Welcome to BunnyNode

BunnyNode is a powerful diagramming and data visualization tool designed for everyone from developers to designers. Whether you are creating flowcharts, UML diagrams, or data visualizations, BunnyNode makes it simple and intuitive.

What is BunnyNode?

BunnyNode combines the simplicity of a whiteboard with the power of a professional diagramming tool. Key highlights include:

  • Intuitive drag-and-drop interface No learning curve required
  • Real-time collaboration Work with your team simultaneously
  • Export PNG (SVG coming soon)
  • Cross-platform Available on Windows (macOS, and Linux coming soon)
